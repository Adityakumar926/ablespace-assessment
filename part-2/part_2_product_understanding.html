1. Executive Summary

AbleSpace is a specialized Special Education (SpEd) management platform engineered for Special Education Teachers, Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs), Occupational Therapists (OTs), and Behavior Specialists (BCBAs). The platform streamlines IEP goal tracking, caseload management, data collection, and compliance reporting.

The "Take Data" button on the Caseload tab serves as the primary operational entry point for practitioners entering instructional or therapy sessions with students.

3. UX/UI & Functional Improvement Recommendations

  • 💡 1. One-Handed Mobile / Tablet Touch Mode: High-contrast touch targets (>60px) for single-tap trial tallying on iPads/tablets.
  • 💡 2. Group Session View (Simultaneous Multi-Student Logging): Side-by-side mini data cards for up to 4 students on one screen.
  • 💡 3. Voice-Assisted Hands-Free Data Entry: Web Speech API voice commands for hands-free trial recording during physical/sensory therapy blocks.
  • 💡 4. Offline-First Local Data Persistence: IndexedDB sync queue ensuring session data is never lost during school Wi-Fi outages.
  • 💡 5. Real-Time IEP Mastery Notifications: Instant visual micro-banners celebrating goal mastery thresholds during active data entry.
